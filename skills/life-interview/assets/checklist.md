# Interview checklist

`[ ]` open, `[x]` covered (date and file), `[~]` declined by the owner. Add a line to cover something new.

## Day 1 — Profile → profile/basics.md
- [ ] d1-identity: full name, how to address you, date of birth, citizenship
- [ ] d1-location: where you live now, time zone, where you are registered
- [ ] d1-languages: languages you speak and how well, the language documents should be in
- [ ] d1-documents: which documents you hold (passport, visa, residence permit, licence), their expiry dates, where the originals and scans are kept
- [ ] d1-contacts: which email, phone and messengers to use for what

## Day 2 — Work → areas/work.md
- [ ] d2-roles: what you do, for whom, in which role
- [ ] d2-projects: current projects and what each one is for
- [ ] d2-schedule: working hours, days off, when you must not be disturbed
- [ ] d2-tools: tools and services your work depends on
- [ ] d2-income: where income comes from and how regular it is

## Day 3 — Money → areas/money.md
- [ ] d3-accounts: which banks, accounts and currencies you use (names only, no numbers)
- [ ] d3-regular: regular payments and subscriptions, their dates and amounts
- [ ] d3-rules: spending rules, limits, what counts as a big purchase
- [ ] d3-taxes: tax status, deadlines, who helps with it
- [ ] d3-pay-how: which payment method to prepare for which kind of purchase

## Day 4 — Health and routine → areas/health.md
- [ ] d4-health: chronic conditions, allergies, medicines, doctors
- [ ] d4-insurance: medical insurance, what it covers, where the policy is kept
- [ ] d4-routine: sleep, sport, a typical day
- [ ] d4-food: diet, what you do not eat, favourite places

## Day 5 — People → people/index-people.md
- [ ] d5-family: family members, how to address them, important dates
- [ ] d5-friends: close friends and what matters about each
- [ ] d5-work-people: colleagues, partners, clients you deal with often
- [ ] d5-services: people who do things for you (accountant, lawyer, doctor, landlord)

## Day 6 — Home, travel, documents → areas/home-travel-documents.md
- [ ] d6-home: housing, rent or ownership, utilities, who to call when something breaks
- [ ] d6-tickets: flights and trains (class, seat, airlines to prefer or avoid, luggage)
- [ ] d6-stay: hotels and flats (budget, area, must-haves)
- [ ] d6-trips: trips planned or repeated every year
- [ ] d6-paperwork: forms and applications you fill in regularly, where the templates are

## Day 7 — Goals and values → areas/goals.md
- [ ] d7-goals: goals for this year and the next three
- [ ] d7-priorities: what comes first when time or money is short
- [ ] d7-autonomy: what the assistant may decide alone and what always needs your yes
- [ ] d7-style: how to report to you (length, tone, when to interrupt)
- [ ] d7-never: things the assistant must never do
